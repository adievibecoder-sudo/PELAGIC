## Decisions and important corrections
1. Retain **native desktop Chrome WebGPU, raw WGSL, browser ES modules and existing custom renderer**. Keep 4× MSAA and create an **opt-in, short-history ocean-aware TAA** experiment with the unmodified MSAA-only path as reference. Standard WebGPU has sample counts 1 and 4, not 2. TAA does not increase texture resolution/physical realism automatically; 4× MSAA + TAA generally adds bandwidth/VRAM and may cost FPS. Temporal cloud reconstruction is a separately measured potential throughput lever.
2. **Pacing first:** published display-paced evidence has ~1000 ms rAF gaps while CPU/queue work is much shorter. Browser/compositor scheduling is the strong lead, *not a proven specific root cause*. Execute the report's small focus/visibility/foreground/background/Chrome/queue-vs-rAF matrix, record physical GPU adapter and distinguish **display FPS** from **GPU throughput** and GPU timestamps. Do not compare temporal motion from the 1 Hz run. Report quantized timestamp precision (~65,536 ns observed) and use appropriate repeat statistics.
3. **Low-Sun missing sun column:** source confirms `sunE = sunIlluminance() * cloudT * waveT.x`; cloud mode differences correlate with disappearing glitter. First run a **temporary diagnostic** replacing cloud attenuation with 1 at an identical low-Sun camera/seed/time; additionally inspect cloud shadow/ray geometry. **Never hard-code cloudT=1 as final physics.**
4. **Mobley reflectance:** the playbook's uniform-sky render MUST NOT be compared directly against Mobley values for specified solar zenith/view azimuth/sky conditions. Use uniform-sky for a self-consistent BRDF/energy test; separately reproduce comparable directional illumination, wind and wavelength before quantitative comparison. Mobley 1999 vs revised 2015 tables have different polarisation/shadow assumptions; document which. The report's ±20% is a *provisional*, not mandatory, acceptance band.
5. **Cox–Munk:** `F.waterCol.w = 5e-5` is the unresolved residual floor only, **not total model mean-square slope**. Compare resolved plus unresolved filtered slope statistics in a consistent wind-height/sea-state and directional convention. Don't simply replace `5e-5` with `0.039`.
6. **Monahan and Koepke:** empirical whitecap percentages (~0.3% for 7 m/s; ~10.5% for 20 m/s) are broad ensemble benchmarks dependent on fetch, stability and wind history; do not hard-code those percentages per frame. Koepke's ~22% is an **effective lifetime-average whitecap reflectance**, not the material's instantaneous shader albedo. Preserve physically coherent fresh/aged foam differences.
7. **Spectrum:** preserve six FFT cascade band/energy/phase contracts. TMA is a finite-depth JONSWAP extension, not inherently superior for deep open ocean. Prototype **frequency-dependent directional spreading and coherent swell organization** only if the existing spectrum and high/low frequency energy budgets show a concrete gap. Re-run Hs/Tp, slope moments, overlap and phase tests.
8. **High-frequency cutoff:** the proposal to move subpixel/capillary normals into filtered roughness is promising but its ~1.5–2 cm and `ωΔt` cutoffs are conditional on frame cadence, dispersion regime, sample footprint and appearance. Avoid one irreversible hard global cutoff; maintain conserved slope variance and check appearance/motion at several frame rates. Preserve/review the already tested f32 slope moments: no meaningful visual gain and about +5.25 ms measured in an earlier opt-in path.
9. **Crest colour:** Sea of Thieves uses a stylized peak-mask/subsurface-colour treatment. Beer–Lambert by itself does not uniquely determine an optically valid illuminated crest without thickness, scattering, view/sun geometry and energy budget. Build an opt-in physically informed candidate and visual control, not an unconditional `0.035` replacement.
10. **B1:** connect onset/compression to localized breaking, foam age/coverage/transport/shape, conservative age-dependent diffusion, holes/lace/wind streaks, bubble/aeration and illuminated spray/spindrift; avoid hard grey decals. No acceptance until a crest-following, weather-responsive result visibly improves. Do not confuse simulation memory with screen coverage; warm up storms before steady-state measurement.
11. **B2:** rain support/front/weather/wind coupling partially exist; current lightning eligibility has no completed light/render pathway. Build spatial rain curtain/visibility, cloud association, physically dimensionalized precipitation, wind-oriented drops, storm aerosol, credible duration/fetch-dependent wind-to-sea transition, and cloud/sea lightning illumination, with separate nonconvective-gale control. The report's 1.5–5 km visibility corresponds to a measured **10 mm/h** event; PELAGIC's normalized rain intensity is not yet mm/h. Do not retune until units are mapped. Avoid adopting multi-hour sea growth as a universal constant without fetch/wind-history/scenario controls.
12. **Clouds:** source-supported `thermalShape` ellipsoid lobes, low basal-ambient scale, bright rims and repeated cell layout motivate bounded morphology changes (vertical profile × multiscale erosion, varied cell dimensions/weather structure, appropriate multiple scatter). World-anchored noise is source-supported and the report found no swimming in sampled sequences, but don't infer that all motion views/curvature are verified. Keep the volumetric custom engine; do not replace with SoT mesh clouds.
13. **Night:** star grid and pole axis, lunar Lambert phase, noncalibrated relative radiometry, fp16 dim-signal quantization, star/cloud occlusion and exposure need attention. Do not convert magnitude/illuminance values directly into the existing arbitrary scene units without a unit/radiometric calibration and GPU readbacks. Keep night improvements scoped to A+B.
14. **WebGPU correctness:** preserve reversed-Z infinite-far projection, 4× MSAA color and depth, sample-coherent velocity, no hardware depth resolve, HDR premultiplication/pre-exposure ratio, only correct resource usage/storage, explicit binding lifetime, reliable shader/pipeline errors, and layout tests. Avoid gratuitous device-system rewrite or arbitrary graphics caps.
15. **Evidence:** the 82-page report says 575 stills reviewed via contact sheets/selected crops, 428 unique and 147 pixel-identical repeats, and only 9 of 61 videos temporally analysed. These are *reported audit results*, not an independently reproduced media census here. Keep historical originals; add duplicate/no-difference warnings, not destructive cleanup. Use a handful of matched **lossless fixed-dt motion captures** for actual TAA/glitter tests rather than a blanket 52-video reshoot.
16. **Research contradictions/precision cautions:** reported 4-phase `±¼` MSAA jitter stratification is a plausible candidate but sample positions/motion/jitter sign need browser tests against recentered Halton. If projected vertex clip coordinates add `jitterNDC * w`, inverse sky ray should compensate with the opposite sign. With jitter-free velocity, historical reprojection needs the current-vs-previous jitter difference. Proposed pixel/subpercent acceptance cutoffs, VRAM ~71 MiB and GPU +0.5–1.5 ms are design estimates rather than validated RTX 3050 numbers. One absolute `lastDisp` copy move needs the foam/particle frame dependency tested, not assumed semantically neutral.
17. **External aids:** `https://github.com/pmndrs/upscaler` offers raw-`GPUDevice` WGSL accumulation/depth/reactive algorithms but is a temporal *upscaler*, expects single-sample inputs and finite far depth; use as an **algorithm reference**, not plug-and-play package or engine migration. `https://github.com/cazala/webgpu-skill` provides framework-agnostic WGSL/WebGPU engineering guardrails; inspect licensing before redistributing its text. No Vite/Three.js migration.
