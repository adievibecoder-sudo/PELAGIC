# PELAGIC 0.4 — Owner visual review and sailing direction (26 October 2026)

Combined Milestones A+B remain the active scope. The owner envisions a future browser sailing experience inspired by Sea of Thieves: an explorable ship, automatic day/night progression, regional evolving weather and a coherent environment. This is long-term direction, not permission to implement a ship or enter Milestone C now. The target deliberately combines physically grounded naturalism and exceptional visual craft; the requested “50/50” is an artistic balance, not literal numerical shader weights.

Five original owner screenshots dated 26 October 2026 show the Storm preset, wind displays of roughly 20–30 m/s and swell height 3.4 m in some views. White and grey streaks dominate large areas of wave faces and obscure readable water forms. The owner reports excessive persistent foam, unattractive water colour and lighting, insufficient storm severity, poor night presentation, and controls/presets that do not consistently respond or communicate their meaning. Daytime clouds have promising visual qualities worth preserving. Screenshots alone cannot prove foam lifetime, controls failing, or whether the visible patterns are actual foam, reflected radiance or optional crest-sheet geometry. Verify source flags and moving captures. A 30 m/s gale can have abundant whitecaps, and wind strength alone need not imply lightning or convective clouds.
