**Feature presence, passing tests, a commit, or an impressive report are NOT successful visual delivery.** Every source change must move the *experienced* ocean/sky/environment closer to credible naturalism **and** beautiful, coherent AAA-grade craftsmanship. **Apply this loop to EVERY A+B visual system and their dependencies—not only whitewater:**
1. **Implement substantively.** Make the best physically reasoned, visual-craft-aware change for a verified deficiency, preserving working foundation and reversible alternatives.
2. **Inspect honestly.** View real continuous motion, side-by-side with an equivalent baseline and appropriate owner references, including the failure-revealing angle, time and sea state. Substantial upgrade/reconstruction is authorized **inside A+B** when supported by visible failure, a causal technical explanation, an evidence-based alternative and an integration/performance plan.
